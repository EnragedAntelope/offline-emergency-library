# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith; critique ideas, not people.
- Help newcomers — many contributors here are testing on a new OS or filing their first PR.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption.
- Sharing pirated or copyrighted content (this project is strictly free/open/public-domain).

## Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately by opening a
**GitHub Security Advisory** on this repository, or by contacting the maintainer through
their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled confidentially.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may ban anyone for behavior they
deem inappropriate. This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
